Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

The short version

  • VocoGogo collects no personal data — none at all.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no profiles.
  • There is no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere even in principle.
  • Your decks and progress live in the app's private storage on your own device.
  • Files you export yourself — a backup or a CSV — go wherever you choose to put them.

This policy covers the VocoGogo Android app (com.miroda.vocogogo) and this website. The detail below simply explains the summary above.

What the app stores

Everything you create stays on your device, written to the app's own private storage area that other apps cannot read:

  • The decks you import — the words, their translations and any group labels.
  • Your study progress — each word's weight, its place in the review schedule and how often it's been shown.
  • Your preferences — timings, weighting settings, palette and light/dark choice.

That's the complete list. No names, no email addresses, no device identifiers, no location, no contacts, no usage statistics.

No network access

VocoGogo does not request the Android INTERNET permission. An app without that permission is blocked by the operating system from making network connections, so your vocabulary and progress cannot leave your device through the app. This isn't just a promise — you can verify it yourself in the app's permission list, which is empty.

Links that open elsewhere

A few buttons in Settings hand off to your browser or the Play Store — the “Buy me a coffee” link, this website, and the Play Store rating link. Once you follow one of those, you're on someone else's site and their privacy policy applies, not this one. VocoGogo sends nothing about you along with the link.

Android backup

This is one of only two ways app data can leave your device — the other being a file you export yourself, described below. Both are worth being clear about.

The app allows Android's built-in Auto Backup. If you have device backup switched on in your Android settings, the operating system may include VocoGogo's data (your decks and progress) in the encrypted backup it makes to your own Google Drive. This is handled entirely by Android and Google, not by VocoGogo — the app never sees or controls that transfer, and the developer has no access to it.

You can turn this off for all apps in your device's backup settings, or by excluding VocoGogo there. Google's handling of backup data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Importing files

When you import a CSV, Android's file picker asks you to choose a file and passes just that one file to the app. VocoGogo reads its contents, converts it into a deck, and stores the result locally. It gets no broader access to your storage and does not keep a reference to the original file.

Files you export

VocoGogo can write two kinds of file at your request: a backup containing your decks, progress and settings, and a CSV export of a single deck's word list. Neither is created unless you ask for it.

Where the file goes is your choice, made in Android's own file picker. If you choose a cloud service such as Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, the file is then held by that service and covered by its privacy policy rather than this one. VocoGogo has no account with any of them and, having no internet permission, no way to reach them itself — it hands the file to the system picker, which writes it only where you point it.

Restoring works the same way in reverse: you pick one file, and the app reads only that file.

Deleting your data

Delete individual words or decks in the app, or remove everything at once by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → VocoGogo → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that erases it completely. If device backup is enabled, you may also want to remove the app's data from your Google backup — and any backup or CSV file you exported yourself stays where you saved it until you delete it there.

Children

VocoGogo is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included. Nothing is knowingly gathered from users of any age, because nothing is gathered at all.

Changes

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before or alongside that release, and the date at the top will change. Any change that introduces data collection would be described here plainly.

Overview

This policy explains what information VocoGogo ("the App") collects, how it's used, and your choices. VocoGogo is built by MirodaLab ("we", "us").

Information We Collect

VocoGogo is designed to work with data you create yourself — the decks, words, and review history you build inside the App. By default, this data is stored locally on your device. If a future version adds optional account creation, cloud backup, or sync, this section should be updated to describe what account information (such as an email address) that feature collects. If pronunciation audio is fetched from a third-party text-to-speech service, this section should also name that provider and link to its privacy policy.

How We Use Information

Data you log in VocoGogo is used only to power the App's own features — showing your decks, scheduling reviews, and tracking stats. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Data Storage & Security

Your decks and review history live on your device unless you explicitly enable a sync or backup feature. If VocoGogo later adds cloud sync, describe here where that data is hosted and how it's protected.

Third-Party Services

This website uses Giscus for comments, which stores comments as GitHub Discussions under GitHub's own privacy policy. The App itself does not currently integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs — update this section if that changes.

Children's Privacy

VocoGogo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
